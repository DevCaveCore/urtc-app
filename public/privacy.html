ÜrTC Privacy Policy

Cave Core Dynamics™ · Effective July 2026 · Contact: admin@cavecoredynamics.org

What we collect

Account information (email, username) when you create an account; location data (with your permission) to show nearby places, weather, and air quality; trip data you create (flights, places, notes, budgets); and payment status via Stripe (we never see or store card numbers).

How we use it

To provide the app's features: live flight tracking, nearby recommendations, trip planning, AI assistance (Apollo), and subscription management. AI conversations are processed by Google Gemini to generate responses. Flight data is provided by third-party aviation data services; weather by OpenWeather; maps and places by Google Maps Platform.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your location with advertisers. Apollo conversations are not used to train AI models by Cave Core Dynamics.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe under their privacy policy. We receive only your subscription status and a customer reference.

Data storage & deletion

Account and trip data are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) in the United States. To delete your account and all associated data, email admin@cavecoredynamics.org from your account email and we will complete deletion within 30 days.

Advertising

Free-tier users may see advertisements. Diamond subscribers see no ads.

Children

ÜrTC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change. Material changes will be announced in the app.
