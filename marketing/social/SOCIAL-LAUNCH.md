## 30-second demo video storyboard

The strongest "AI video" is a real screen recording — authentic beats generated.
Record the app (QuickTime on Mac: File → New Screen Recording over the phone
simulator or the browser at 390×844) and follow this shot list:

1. **0–4s** — Today tab. Cursor taps the Apollo orb.
2. **4–12s** — Type: "I need to get to LA next Friday, under $1,000." Apollo replies with real ranked fares.
3. **12–20s** — Flights tab → Book Travel. The search is already prefilled. Tap Find My Flights → Apollo's Recommendations appear with airline logos and "Why Apollo picked this one".
4. **20–26s** — Tap Book → checkout → Booking Confirmed 🎉 with the confirmation code.
5. **26–30s** — Trips tab showing the trip auto-created. End card: promo-card1 with the URL.

Add captions in CapCut (auto-captions work well), keep the app's own dark UI as
the backdrop, no music louder than -18dB. Export 1080×1920 for the pinned
version, 16:9 for the thread.

Tip: for AI-generated b-roll (drone city shots between screens), Veo or Runway
work — prompt "cinematic aerial dusk shot of Los Angeles skyline, warm orange
haze" to match the brand palette. Keep it to 2–3 seconds max between UI shots.
